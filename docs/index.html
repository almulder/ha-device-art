Device Art Card

Power Scenery Show Mode
Every drawing in the card. Pick a category on the left, try the power switch, slider, colors and menus, then press Copy YAML and paste it into a manual card in your dashboard. Install the card with HACS (or add ha-device-art.js as a dashboard resource), then set entity to your own device.
Multi-function drawings (the N parts badge) can follow one entity per part: tick only the parts your device has, open each door or drawer, pick a stage (cycle, mode, state) and set the readings. Switch Mode to One device for the classic single-entity look.
New in 1.3.5: map icons for people on the move: car, sports car, truck, SUV, off-road 4x4, van, bike, semi truck, motorcycle, and walking person, woman and child; any color, facing right or left (pick Show: New in 1.3.5).
New in 1.3.4: garage doors get Windows (none, top, bottom, down the right or left, everywhere), Panels (4 or 5), Window shape, Glass and Texture menus.
New in 1.3.3: chicken lamps (a ceramic hen, a rooster lamp, a chick globe and a hen with chicks) that glow like a bulb when on; pick the chicken's color and the light color, and a base on three of them (pick Show: New in 1.3.3).
New in 1.3.1: display cabinet lights (curio, corner curio, china hutch, glass tower, wall glass cabinet, wine bar and a bookcase with LED) with a light type, shelves, contents, light color and frame finish, and the glass tower lights each shelf on its own; six ceiling fans also take a Bowl, Bar or Drum light kit (pick Show: New in 1.3.1).
New in 1.3.0: relays and modules, pergolas and radiator valves, buttons and remotes, grow, UV and emergency lights, leak sensors, and builders for smoke and CO alarms, video doorbells, garage doors, doors and windows (pick Show: New in 1.3.0). Many drawings also got new menus: lock states, HVAC mode, preset and swing, camera states, switch gangs, plug meters and more. Every menu and color can be mixed, so there are far more looks than any picture shows.
